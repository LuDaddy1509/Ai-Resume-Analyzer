- **Developer count**: ~500k+ (2024 estimate)
- **Major hubs**: Hanoi, Ho Chi Minh City, Da Nang

## Interview Process (Typical)
1. **Screen**: 15-30 min (HR/Recruiter)
2. **Technical**: 1-2 rounds (Live coding / Take-home / System Design)
3. **Manager/Team**: 45-60 min (Behavioral + Technical depth)
4. **CTO/VP** (Senior+): Strategic fit, culture
5. **Offer**: 1-2 weeks after final round

## Negotiation Norms (VN Context)
- **Base salary**: Negotiable 10-20% for senior roles
- **Equity**: Rare in outsourcing, common in product startups
- **Benefits standard**: 13th month salary, health insurance (family), lunch, gym, learning budget $500-2000/yr
- **Probation**: 2 months (Labor Code), can negotiate 1 month
- **Notice period**: 30 days (standard), 45 days for managers

## Tax & Legal
- **PIT (Personal Income Tax)**: Progressive 5-35%
- **Social Insurance**: 10.5% (employer 17.5%, employee 8%) - capped at 2024 rates
- **Health Insurance**: 4.5% (employer 3%, employee 1.5%)
- **Unemployment**: 1% each
- **Total employer cost**: ~26% on top of gross
- **Foreign remote**: Usually contractor (B2B invoice) - self-tax, no benefits

## Career Paths
1. **IC Track**: Junior → Mid → Senior → Staff → Principal → Distinguished
2. **Management**: Senior → Tech Lead → Engineering Manager → Director → VP
3. **Specialist**: Security, Data, AI/ML, Platform, Architecture
4. **Entrepreneurship**: Founder, CTO, Freelance/Consultant
