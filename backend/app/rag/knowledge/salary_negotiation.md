# Salary Negotiation & Offer Evaluation Guide

## Total Compensation Components

### Base Salary
- **Market rate**: Check levels.fyi, Glassdoor, Blind, H1B data, Company-specific threads
- **Location differential**: SF/NYC +30-50%, Remote -10-20% vs HQ
- **Band width**: Typically 20-30% range within level (e.g., L5: $180-230k)

### Equity (RSU/Stock Options)
| Company Stage | Typical Grant (Senior) | Vesting | Refresh |
|---------------|------------------------|---------|---------|
| Public (FAANG) | $100-300k/yr | 4yr monthly/quarterly | Annual |
| Late Unicorn | $50-150k/yr | 4yr quarterly | Annual |
| Series C-D | $30-100k/yr | 4yr quarterly | Maybe |
| Series A-B | $10-50k/yr | 4yr monthly | Rare |
| Seed | 0.1-1% | 4yr monthly | None |

**Key Questions**:
- Current 409A valuation? Last funding round price?
- Outstanding shares? Fully diluted %?
- Liquidation preference? (1x non-participating standard)
- Acceleration on acquisition? (Single/double trigger)

### Bonus
- **Annual Bonus**: 10-20% base (target), paid if company/individual targets met
- **Sign-on Bonus**: $10-100k (clawback 12-24 months)
- **Spot/Retention**: Ad-hoc, variable

### Benefits Valuation (Annual $ Equivalent)
- **Health Insurance**: $8-20k (family vs individual)
- **401k Match**: 4-6% base = $7-15k
- **ESPP**: 10-15% discount = $3-8k
- **Learning Budget**: $1-5k
- **Remote/Co-working**: $3-10k
- **Parental Leave**: 12-26 weeks paid
- **PTO**: 15-30 days + holidays

## Negotiation Framework

### Preparation (Before Offer)
1. **BATNA**: Best Alternative to Negotiated Agreement (current job, other offers)
2. **Anchor Research**: 3+ data points for target role/level/location
3. **Priority Ranking**: Base > Equity > Bonus > Benefits > Sign-on > Title > Start date

### The Conversation
**Recruiter**: "What are your salary expectations?"
**You**: "Based on my research for [Role] at [Level] in [Location], the market range is $X-Y. Given my experience in [specific relevant experience], I'm targeting the top of that band. What's the budgeted range for this role?"

**If they push first**: "I'd prefer to understand the range first to ensure alignment. What's the approved band for this level?"

### Negotiation Levers (in priority order)
1. **Competing Offer**: "I have another offer at $X base + $Y equity. I prefer your company because [reason], can you match?"
2. **Market Data**: "Levels.fyi shows median $X for this role/level. Your offer is at 25th percentile."
3. **Unique Value**: "My experience in [exact tech stack/domain] means I can contribute immediately on [key project]."
4. **Equity Upside**: "Can we increase RSU grant? I'm long-term oriented."
5. **Sign-on**: "To bridge gap, can you add $X sign-on (offsets year 1 equity cliff)?"

### Common Objections & Responses
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Budget is fixed" | "Understood. Can we explore equity/sign-on/title/start date flexibility?" |
| "Equity is standardized" | "What about a one-time equity refresh or increased refresh rate?" |
| "Need approval" | "What's the process? Can I speak with hiring manager?" |
| "Other candidates at this rate" | "I bring [unique differentiator]. What would it take to get to $X?" |

## Offer Evaluation Checklist

### Financial (Score 1-10 each)
- [ ] Base salary vs market percentile
- [ ] Equity grant value (conservative 409A × shares)
- [ ] Equity growth potential (company trajectory)
- [ ] Bonus target + historical payout %
- [ ] Sign-on (net of clawback)
- [ ] Benefits total value
- **Total Year 1 Cash**: Base + Bonus + Sign-on
- **Total 4-Year Value**: 4×Base + 4×Bonus + Equity + Benefits

### Non-Financial
- [ ] Role scope & autonomy
- [ ] Manager quality (ask team: "What's it like working for X?")
- [ ] Team culture & psychological safety
- [ ] Learning/growth opportunities
- [ ] WLB reality (ask: "Typical week? On-call?")
- [ ] Remote/hybrid policy (in writing)
- [ ] Tech stack alignment with interests
- [ ] Company trajectory (funding, revenue, market position)

### Red Flags in Offer
- Exploding offer (< 1 week)
- No equity refresh policy
- "Unlimited PTO" without minimum days tracked
- Vague promotion criteria
- High equity % but low base (cash flow risk)
- Clawback > 12 months for sign-on
- No 401k match / poor health plan

## Negotiation Email Template

```
Subject: Offer Discussion - [Role] - [Your Name]

Hi [Recruiter],

Thank you for the offer. I'm excited about [company] and the [specific project/team] opportunity.

After reviewing, I'd like to discuss the compensation package. Based on my research for [Role/Level] in [Location], market data shows:
- Base: $X-Y (median $Z) — Offer: $A
- Equity: $X-Y/yr — Offer: $B/yr
- Total 4-yr: $X — Offer: $Y

Given my [specific experience: e.g., "5 years building payment systems at scale with Go/Kubernetes"], I believe I can deliver outsized impact on [specific initiative mentioned in interviews].

Could we explore:
1. Base: $X (market median)
2. Equity: $Y/yr (or +Z shares)
3. Sign-on: $Z to bridge year 1 gap

I'm flexible on the mix and want to find a package that works for both sides. Available to chat this week.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

## When to Walk Away
- Company refuses any negotiation (signals rigid culture)
- Offer < 25th percentile with no justification
- Major red flags in team/culture discovered
- Better BATNA exists (other offer, current role growth)
- Gut feel: "Something's off" after multiple conversations
